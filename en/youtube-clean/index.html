Other URLs (Remove Generic Parameters)

For non-YouTube URLs, this removes tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, si, and so on).
To avoid breaking parameters a page needs to work, only known tracking parameters are removed and everything else is left as it is.
Amazon URLs are a special case: they are cut down to the shortest form, amazon.co.jp/dp/ASIN, keeping only the product ID (ASIN).

What is removed and what is kept (YouTube)

  • Kept: the video ID and the start time (t= or start=). Forms like 1m30s are normalized to seconds (90).
  • Removed: si= (share tracking), pp=, feature=, ab_channel=, list= / index= (playlist context), utm_*, and everything else that isn't needed to play the video.
  • Supported URL types: watch, youtu.be, shorts, live, embed, and the m. and music. hosts.
  • Short URL (youtu.be): off gives youtube.com/watch?v=ID&t=90s (the long form, with an s); on gives youtu.be/ID?t=90 (youtu.be, no s — the same form as YouTube's own share links).
  • Remove the start time (t=): when on, the start position is dropped and you get a URL for the video only.
