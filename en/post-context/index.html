X Post Context Search

Paste the URL of a post on X and this tool generates a search command and link that shows what the author said before and after that post.
It works back from the post ID to the exact time the post was made, so you never have to type a date. The range is centered on the moment of the post, to the second.

When this is useful

  • You see a post and wonder, "What else was this person saying around that day?" You can follow the whole flow of what they posted before and after it.
  • You only see a comment on a repost, something like "RP > ...", and can't tell what was reposted. Turn on "Include reposts" and search: the repost appears in the timeline at the time it was reposted, so you can identify what the comment was about. (This works around X's UI change that shows reposts and posts separately and makes it hard to read comments made after reposting.)

How it works

Every X post ID has its posting time embedded in it, down to the millisecond. This tool works the time back out of the ID and passes it to the search operators since: and until: as a date and time in Japan Standard Time (in a format like 2026-07-15_15:32:28_JST) to cut out the surrounding period.
Because _JST is written explicitly, the result doesn't depend on any time zone difference with UTC, and the range is exact to the second, centered on the moment of the post. Since the dates are plain date-times, you can widen or narrow the range afterward just by editing the numbers in the search box.
With "Output in Unix seconds" on, the command uses since_time: / until_time: (Unix seconds) instead. Precision and time zone handling are the same.
Note that the search itself runs on X, so posts from private accounts and deleted posts do not appear. The username in the URL may differ from the current one (for example after a rename).
