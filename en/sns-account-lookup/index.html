Find the Account from a Post URL

Enter the URL (or ID) of an Instagram, X, Threads or TikTok post to see the current username, today's URL and the time it was posted. Put one per line; you can look up to 5 at a time.
This tool makes network requests. The URLs and IDs you enter are sent through this site's server to Instagram, X, Threads and TikTok to look them up. What you enter is not stored. See How to use it & how it works for details.
