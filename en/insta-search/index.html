Search Your Own Instagram Posts

This runs only inside your browser. Your data is never sent anywhere.

When you choose a file or folder, your browser may ask whether to "upload" it. That is just the browser's standard wording. In fact nothing is sent anywhere; this page only reads what is inside.

How to export your data
  1. On a computer: the menu (three lines, "More") on the left > Settings > Meta Accounts Center at the top > Your information and permissions > Export your information > Create export
  2. Your Instagram account > Export to device
  3. Customize information: press "Clear all", then check only "Media" under "Your Instagram activity"
  4. Date range: All time; format: JSON; media quality: your choice (low quality is faster if you only want to search)
  5. When the notification arrives, download the ZIP and choose it here without unzipping. If it is split into several ZIPs, you can choose them all at once
  6. Android app: three lines at the top right > Meta Accounts Center, then choose the same options
To open a post you found, set its date in Instagram's Activity page (steps). The date in the app can be one day off.
